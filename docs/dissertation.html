Headline Audit Summary

Every numbered theorem, proposition, lemma, corollary, definition, example, and remark in Chapters 1–3 of the dissertation has a sorry-free Lean 4 verification (lake build, 3390 jobs in Lean v4.35.0). Appendices A, B, and D—plus the general even-\(n\) isomorphism \(W(\widetilde{A_{-n}}) \cong W(D_{n+1})\)—are verified in Python (pytest).
